Lockdown rules

  1. The exam opens in full screen. The clock starts the moment you press Begin and does not pause.
  2. Leaving the exam tab, switching windows or exiting full screen is recorded as a violation. After 3 violations the exam submits itself.
  3. Copy, cut, paste, drag-and-drop and right-click are disabled. Everything must be typed.
  4. If you close or refresh the page by accident, reopen it: your answers and the clock continue from where they were.
  5. When the clock reaches 00:00 the exam submits automatically with whatever you have written.
  6. After submission, your writing is marked out of 40 against the EoT1 rubric and you get a breakdown with corrections.
One of three Smart Homes sets is chosen at random.
